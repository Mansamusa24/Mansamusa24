SLIDE 2 — What Is Market Structure?

— Titus —
02 / 06
What is
Market Structure?
Market structure is how price moves through the market — it tells you whether buyers or sellers are in control at any given moment.
📈
Uptrend — Higher Highs + Higher Lows. Buyers in control.
📉
Downtrend — Lower Highs + Lower Lows. Sellers in control.
⚠️
Ranging — Price bouncing between the same highs and lows.
Master this — and you'll always know who is winning before you enter.

SLIDE 3 — Break of Structure

— Titus —
03 / 06
Break of
Structure (BOS)
A Break of Structure is when price breaks a key swing high or low — signalling a potential shift in trend.
🔼
Bullish BOS — Price breaks above a previous swing high. Look for buys.
🔽
Bearish BOS — Price breaks below a previous swing low. Look for sells.
⚡
CHoCH — Change of Character. First sign the trend is reversing.
BOS = confirmation. CHoCH = warning. Know the difference.

How to
Trade It
Don't just spot the structure — trade it with a clear process.
1️⃣
Identify the current trend on the higher timeframe (H4 or Daily).
2️⃣
Wait for a BOS or CHoCH on the lower timeframe (15m or 1H).
3️⃣
Enter on the retest of the broken structure level.
4️⃣
Set SL below the swing low. Target the next key level.
